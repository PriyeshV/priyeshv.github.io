A connecting perspective

How does information travel?

Graph propagation methods make the flow of neighborhood information explicit. My work on higher-order propagation and graph convolutional networks examines how models combine information across multiple hops.

In reinforcement learning, I study structural representations and temporally extended actions through Laplacian representations and hierarchical eigen options. These papers form a second strand of my research on learning and decision-making.

Careful evaluation is a third strand: our network representation benchmark examines whether apparent advances persist under systematic comparisons.

Developing direction

From graph structure to language models.

My emerging interests include LLM internals, information propagation, and generalization. I’m interested in asking how ideas from graph representations and structured computation might help study these systems. These are research interests, rather than claims of an established LLM publication record.
