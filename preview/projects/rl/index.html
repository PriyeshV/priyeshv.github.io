Reinforcement learning

Structure for exploration.

Hierarchical options and Laplacian representations for decision-making.

The research question

How can representations of an environment’s structure support learning when rewards are sparse? My recent work examines hierarchical eigen options and Laplacian representations in deep RL.

Hierarchical eigen options

“Addressing Sparse-Rewards in RL with Scalable Hierarchical Novel Eigen Options” studies scalable hierarchical options for sparse-reward RL. The supplied sources report acceptance at NeurIPS 2026 and a spotlight at the RLC 2026 Reinforcement Learning Beyond Rewards Workshop. The available link points to the workshop version.

Laplacian representations

“Revisiting Laplacian Representations for Value Function Approximation in Deep RL” examines Laplacian representations for value function approximation. It was presented at the Inductive Biases in Reinforcement Learning Workshop at RLC 2025.

Learning to sample a graph

Earlier collaborative work used reinforcement learning for effective sampling in unknown social networks, in the context of influence maximization. The AAMAS 2020 paper was nominated for a Best Paper Award.
