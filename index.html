About me

Connecting representations
and decisions.

I’m a PhD candidate in Computer Science at McGill University and Mila, supervised by Doina Precup and Samira Ebrahimi Kahou.

My work spans reinforcement learning, deep learning, and complex networks. I model irregularly structured domains as graphs and investigate representations and learning methods for effective inference.

I completed my MS by Research at IIT Madras with Balaraman Ravindran, and spent five years as research staff at RISE Lab and RBCDSAI.
