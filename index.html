About me

Connecting representations
and decisions.

I’m a PhD candidate in Computer Science at McGill University and Mila, advised by Doina Precup and Samira E. Kahou. Previously, I completed my MS by Research at IIT Madras under the supervision of Balaraman Ravindran, where I also worked as a researcher at the Robert Bosch Centre for Data Science and AI and the RISE Lab.

My research focuses on reinforcement learning and representation learning, particularly on exploiting the underlying structure of learning problems to improve representations, generalization, and decision-making. My PhD research investigates spectral representations of environment dynamics and their applications to value function approximation and exploration in deep reinforcement learning. My earlier work explored graph neural networks, graph representation learning, and learning from structured data.

More broadly, I am interested in large language models (LLMs), reasoning, and AI for Science. I am particularly interested in how advances in representation learning and reinforcement learning can contribute to more capable, generalizable AI systems and accelerate scientific discovery.
