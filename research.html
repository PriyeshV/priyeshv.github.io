A connecting perspective

How does information travel?

Graph propagation methods make the flow of neighborhood information explicit. My work on higher-order propagation and graph convolutional networks examines how models combine information across multiple hops.

In reinforcement learning, I study structural representations and temporally extended actions through Laplacian representations and hierarchical eigen options. These papers form a second strand of my research on learning and decision-making.

Careful evaluation is a third strand: our network representation benchmark examines whether apparent advances persist under systematic comparisons.

Research interests

Research directions.

Building on my background in reinforcement learning, representation learning, and graph-based learning, I am interested in exploring the following research directions:

Large Language Models and Reasoning

Understanding and improving the reasoning and generalization capabilities of large language models, including learning from feedback, structured reasoning, and methods for improving reasoning and decision-making.

AI for Science

Developing and applying machine learning methods to scientific discovery, particularly through foundation models, structured representations, and learning-based approaches to modeling and understanding complex systems.

Reinforcement Learning and Foundation Models

Exploring how reinforcement learning and sequential decision-making can improve the training and capabilities of foundation models and autonomous AI systems.
