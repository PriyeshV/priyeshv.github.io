A connecting perspective

Learning with structure.

In reinforcement learning, the graph of environment dynamics is not given explicitly. I study learning Laplacian representations from experience and using them for value function approximation and temporally extended exploration.

In graph-based learning, relational structure is part of the problem. My work examines how models combine information across neighborhoods and relations, preserve node information, and perform under systematic evaluation.

Applications connect these methods to concrete tasks, including dialogue, scene understanding, and decision-making in unknown social networks.
